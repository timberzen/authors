ABOUT THIS BOOK

Book description

How can you care for others while renewing your own inner strength?

Serving others can bring deep meaning, yet leave little room to replenish your own energy. Inspired by Nichiren Buddhism, Manifesting Buddha offers a practical, step-by-step approach to bringing service, self-care, and purposeful change into everyday life.

Doug R. Thompson explores manifestation through intention, spiritual practice, and meaningful action. Chanting, reflection, visualization, and daily rituals provide ways to examine your beliefs, align your choices with your values, and nurture the compassion that sustains your contribution to others.

Through practical exercises, reflection prompts, and stories of modern Bodhisattvas, the book explores how to:

  • Work with the seven-step Manifesting Buddha framework to pursue change in your life and community.
  • Explore chanting Nam-myoho-renge-kyo as a daily practice for renewal and resilience.
  • Recognize limiting beliefs and set intentions that connect your values with your actions.
  • Make space for reflection through a personal manifestation altar, visualization, and guided meditation.
  • Balance service to others with self-nourishment and care for your own well-being.
  • Integrate the book's practices into busy daily routines, including coaching, teaching, or supporting others.
  • Build a supportive community and explore Kosen-Rufu, presented through the aspiration for global harmony.

The book approaches sustainable change as a process that involves timing, effort, and consistent practice. Its accessible steps invite readers to bring both curiosity and critical thought to their spiritual journey.

Whether you are new to Buddhist practice or have explored other paths, Manifesting Buddha offers encouragement for renewing your sense of purpose and expressing compassion in action. It invites you to care for the life within you as you contribute to the lives around you.
