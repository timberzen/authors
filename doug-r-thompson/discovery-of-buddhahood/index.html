ABOUT THIS BOOK

Book description

What if the person you are struggling to become is already within you, waiting to be discovered?

Self-doubt, past mistakes, and difficult circumstances can make it hard to see our own potential. Drawing on the Lotus Sutra and Nichiren Buddhism, The Discovery of Buddhahood: Why Enlightenment Is Closer Than You Think explores the teaching that Buddhahood is an inherent possibility within every life, including our own.

Doug R. Thompson asks what recognizing this Buddha-nature means in practice: how it can change the way we understand ourselves, respond to adversity, approach relationships, and contribute to the happiness of others. Everyday struggles, disappointments, and doubts become the starting point for exploring courage, wisdom, compassion, and purpose.

Through relatable experiences, practical reflections, and accessible explanations of Buddhist philosophy, the book explores how to:

  • Recognize your inherent worth and challenge the self-doubt that obscures your potential.
  • Understand karma and why your past need not determine your future.
  • Meet obstacles with courage and find opportunities for growth in adversity.
  • Recognize beliefs and habitual reactions that undermine confidence and happiness.
  • Explore the Lotus Sutra's teaching that Buddhahood is possible for ordinary people living ordinary lives.
  • Bring wisdom, compassion, and personal responsibility into everyday relationships and decisions.
  • Discover the Bodhisattva within and use your experiences to help others recognize their potential.

Written for practicing Buddhists, those exploring spirituality for the first time, and readers seeking a more empowering understanding of their circumstances, this book offers a way to approach life with greater hope. It invites you to recognize that imperfections do not disqualify you from enlightenment, and to begin expressing your Buddha-nature in the life you are living now.
