THE AUTHOR

About Doug R. Thompson

Doug R. Thompson writes about Nichiren Buddhism, the Lotus Sutra, karma, compassion, and the transformative possibilities of daily practice. His approach emphasizes lived experience, accessible explanations, and how Buddhist understanding takes shape through our choices and relationships.
