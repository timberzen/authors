THE AUTHOR

About Doug R. Thompson

Doug R. Thompson is an author, technologist, and lifelong student of personal and societal transformation. His books explore a central question: how do we remain capable, purposeful, and human in a world that is changing faster than ever before?

His work spans three connected areas.

In his books on Buddhism and personal development, Doug explores the potential for transformation within ordinary human life—how belief, practice, experience, and action can change the way we see ourselves and what we believe is possible.

His technology books draw on decades of experience in software development, systems thinking, product design, and digital technology. Rather than treating technology simply as a collection of tools, he is interested in how better systems can extend human capability and remove unnecessary friction.

His writing on artificial intelligence, accelerating technology, and the future examines a larger question: what happens when humanity's ability to create, communicate, discover, and act begins to accelerate exponentially? These books explore not only what technology can do, but who benefits from it, who determines its direction, and how individuals can participate in the opportunities it creates.

Whether writing about Buddhism software, artificial intelligence, or the future of society, Doug's work is concerned with helping people understand the forces shaping their lives—and use that understanding to become more capable participants in their own future.
